## PlaneIsland

### PlaneIsland? What is this?
    PlaneIsland is a game where you can explore an island with must-see spots. With your little plane, travel around the island, find these spots, and either shoot or fly through them to score points.
Good luck!

### Controls sheet
    - Mouse: Control plane direction
    - Hold Right Click: Stop engine
    - Hold Left Click: Shoot missiles
    - Middle Mouse Button: Boost (when available)
    - Q: Exit Game
